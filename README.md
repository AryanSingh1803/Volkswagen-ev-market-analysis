# Why Volkswagen Dominates China but Struggles in the U.S.
### A Data + Strategy Breakdown

---

## The Question

Volkswagen is the world's second-largest automaker by volume — yet its performance tells two completely different stories depending on which side of the Pacific you're standing on.

In China, VW moves **over 3 million vehicles per year**, commands roughly 15% market share, and has been the dominant foreign brand for nearly four decades. In the United States, it holds a modest **~4% market share** — smaller than Subaru, trailing far behind Toyota, Ford, and GM — and has never meaningfully broken into the American mainstream.

Same brand. Same engineering heritage. Opposite outcomes.

This project investigates why.

---

## What This Analysis Covers

Three interconnected lenses on VW's global strategy:

| Angle | Questions Asked |
|---|---|
| **Sales & Market Share** | How do VW's sales volumes and market positions compare in the US vs China over the last decade? Who are the real competitors in each market? |
| **EV Adoption & Strategy** | Did VW's EV push (ID. series) land differently in each market? How did government policy shape outcomes? |
| **Culture & Consumer Behavior** | Why do Chinese consumers embrace VW while American buyers stay lukewarm? What role does brand perception, localization, and trust play? |
